# Governance and stewardship

The workflow records four decisions:

- source acceptance;
- licence acceptance;
- horticultural assertion review;
- release approval.

Source/licence review may be independent of horticultural review. A record is releasable only when both decisions are accepted.

During the sole-maintainer phase, one maintainer may perform every role, including reviewing their own import. No second reviewer is required. The decisions remain explicit and auditable rather than being inferred from authorship or merge status.

Corrections create a new assertion or review decision and preserve the old value. Releases are immutable; later corrections may supersede records but never rewrite an earlier release.
