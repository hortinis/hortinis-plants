# Cultivation context

Every cultivation claim must state the conditions under which it applies.

Possible context dimensions are:

- geography or climate region;
- outdoor, unheated shelter, heated greenhouse or indoor system;
- direct sowing, transplanting or vegetative propagation;
- cultivar or cultivar group;
- life stage;
- soil or container context;
- irrigation or protection assumptions.

Missing context is `unknown`. Never present a broad source claim as France-specific or cultivar-specific.
