# Licensing policy

## Release gates

- CC0 and public domain: allowed.
- CC BY and approved Open Licences: allowed with attribution.
- CC BY-SA: quarantined until ShareAlike obligations are explicitly accepted for the target artifact.
- CC BY-NC: forbidden in commercial release profiles.
- Unknown or unsupported rights: forbidden.

Metadata rights do not automatically cover an underlying publication, image, quotation or linked page. Images are separate records with separate rights.

Each release must contain an attribution manifest with source, version, URL, licence, required notice and included records. The compiler must fail on forbidden or unknown assertions.

Practical Plants NC blocks must remain distinguishable throughout extraction. CropGraph's declared licence is not sufficient when its source field does not identify the underlying work.
